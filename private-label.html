Process

Private Label Step by Step

1
Choose product category and models
Browse the catalog or contact us. We advise on which models are available for private label and confirm availability.
2
Confirm MOQ and branding requirements
We confirm minimum quantities, available customisation options and pricing based on your requirements.
3
Send brand files and label requirements
Provide your logo files (vector format preferred), label text and any specific requirements for packaging.
4
Approve sample or mockup
We produce a physical sample or digital mockup for your approval before full production begins. We do not start production without written approval.
5
Confirm proforma invoice and pay
A proforma invoice is issued with all confirmed specifications. A deposit may be required before production. Balance is due before dispatch.
6
Production and quality control
Production begins. Standard lead time is 3–4 weeks depending on order size. QC check is performed before packing.
7
Packing and shipping
Order is packed with your branded packaging, export documents are prepared and order is dispatched with tracking.
