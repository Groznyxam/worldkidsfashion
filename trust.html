Quality Control

Pre-Shipment Quality Check

Every wholesale order is checked before packing and dispatch. The following steps apply to standard stock orders:

1
Quantity verification
All items are counted against the confirmed order. Quantities are verified before packing.
2
Size and label check
Size breakdown is verified against the order confirmation. Labels and hangtags are checked if applicable.
3
Visible defect inspection
Products are inspected for visible manufacturing defects, stains, damaged stitching or printing issues.
4
Packing and documentation
Order is packed, packing list is prepared, commercial invoice and export documents are issued.
5
Photo confirmation (on request)
For orders above a certain value or on buyer request, packing photos are provided before dispatch.

Buyer Protection

Claim & Dispute Process

How to file a claim

Step 1: Contact us within 3 business days
Email info@worldkidsfashion.org or WhatsApp. Include your order number.
Step 2: Provide documentation
Photos or video of the issue. Keep original packaging where possible.
Step 3: We review
Our team reviews the claim within 3–5 business days and proposes a resolution.
Step 4: Resolution
Replacement, store credit or agreed refund depending on the nature of the claim.

Valid claims cover

  • Wrong item delivered
  • Wrong size delivered
  • Missing quantity
  • Visible manufacturing defect
  • Damage caused before dispatch

Claims do not cover

  • ✕ Unsold inventory
  • ✕ Change of mind after shipment
  • ✕ Incorrect size selection by buyer
  • ✕ Customs delays or import refusal
  • ✕ Local import charges
