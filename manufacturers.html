Pre-Vetted Buyers

Export VAT Recovery

Every order through us is an export transaction — you reclaim your input VAT (KDV), a benefit you do not get on domestic sales.

Order Aggregation

We combine multiple small buyers into single production runs, improving your batch efficiency.

Translation Support

Private Label Growth

Access private label opportunities with our brand partners — stable, repeat, long-term volumes.

Turn Domestic Production Into Export Operations

When you fulfill orders through WorldKidsFashion, you sell to our internationally-registered company — making each order a VAT-recoverable export. This is a tax advantage you simply do not have on domestic Turkish sales.
