Our Story

From Istanbul to
120 Countries

WorldKidsFashion™ was founded with a single mission: to connect the world's best Turkish kids & baby clothing manufacturers directly with resellers around the globe — eliminating middlemen, reducing costs, and empowering entrepreneurs.

We are based in Istanbul — the heart of Turkey's world-class textile industry. Our warehouse is strategically located to serve all major shipping routes to Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.

Today, 20,000+ resellers in 120+ countries trust WKF as their primary source for wholesale kids fashion. From boutique owners in Poland to Amazon sellers in the USA — our community is global.
