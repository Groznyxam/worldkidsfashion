About WorldKidsFashion

Wholesale Kidswear Sourcing from Istanbul

WorldKidsFashion helps wholesale buyers source kids and baby clothing from Istanbul-based Turkish manufacturers.

We work with resellers, boutiques, online stores, distributors and growing private label brands that need access to Turkish kidswear production, ready stock and international shipping support.

Our role is simple: to make wholesale sourcing from Turkey easier, clearer and safer for international buyers.

What We Do

  • Ready-stock wholesale kidswear with MOQ from 6 pcs per model
  • Private label production with custom labels, hangtags and packaging
  • Custom kidswear manufacturing — your designs, your sizing
  • Organic and certified fabric options where available and documentable
  • Export packing and international shipping coordination
  • Proforma invoice before every payment
  • Quality control check before dispatch
  • Support for boutiques, online stores and distributors in multiple languages

What We Do Not Do

  • ✕ We do not sell retail quantities to private consumers
  • ✕ We do not claim every product is certified unless certificate documents are available and verifiable
  • ✕ We do not guarantee customs duties or import taxes in the buyer's country
  • ✕ We do not start private label or custom production without written confirmation of specifications
  • ✕ We do not promise delivery times outside of our courier partners' standard estimates
  • ✕ We do not accept returns for unsold inventory, change of mind or size selection errors
